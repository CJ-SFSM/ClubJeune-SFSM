# Our webinars this year

## Webinar schedule for 2025/2026

Since 2024, our webinar seasons have explored a wide range of mass spectrometry topics. We are continuing this momentum in 2025/2026, with the full schedule of online events below.

![](/uploads/actualite/webinaires_2026.png)

## Who can take part?

All CJSM and SFSM members can attend. Simply make sure you are on the club's mailing list to receive the joining link.

## Interested in giving a presentation?

If an event topic relates to your work and you would like to present, please contact us. You will need to send us a title and an abstract of your presentation in English.

**IMPORTANT:**

If you are a student, make sure your supervisors approve your presentation materials and scientific content.
