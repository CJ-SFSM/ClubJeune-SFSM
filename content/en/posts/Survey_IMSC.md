## IMSC 2026

As part of preparations for IMSC 2026 in Lyon, from 22 to 28 August 2026, we sent you a form to learn which course topics interest you. The first two days of the conference are dedicated to young mass spectrometrists, with courses taught by specialists in their fields.

Thank you for taking part. See you in Lyon for IMSC, from **22 to 28 August 2026**.
