## Should we change the name?

We offered you several options for naming the CJSM. More than 50% of voters chose **to keep** the current name. We will therefore remain the Club Jeune de Spectrométrie de Masse (Young Scientists' Club for Mass Spectrometry).

## Survey results

- 34 votes for “Keep the current name”.
- 16 votes for “SFSM-YS: Young Scientists”.
- 13 votes for “RJ-SFSF: Réseau Jeune de la SFSM” (SFSM Young Scientists' Network).
- 4 votes for “SFSM-JT: SFSM – Jeunes Talents” (SFSM – Young Talents).
- 7 blank votes.
