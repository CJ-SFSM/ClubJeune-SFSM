## Rencontres Club Jeune de la Spectrométrie de Masse (RCJSM) - 2027

Join us for the **2027 edition of the Rencontres Club Jeune de la Spectrométrie de Masse (RCJSM)**! 

The meeting will take place from **Monday 1 to Friday 5 March 2027 at Blegny-Mine, near Liège, Belgium**.

Each year, the RCJSM bring together around 40 early-career researchers to share their work, discover new approaches in mass spectrometry and get to know one another in a relaxed, friendly setting.

Holding this year’s meeting in Belgium gives us the chance to celebrate the Belgian members of our community. The meeting will be held in French and English. To help colleagues who do not speak French follow the talks and join the discussions, we invite you to prepare your slides in English. If you feel comfortable speaking English, we encourage you to use it for your talk and during discussions. Talks and questions in French are also welcome.


## What’s on the program ?

- 🧠 Courses and workshops led by mass spectrometry specialists
- 🎤 Talks “by you, for you” where you can share your research and discuss it with fellow Club members
- 🤝 Opportunities to meet our sponsors
- 🎲 Games and social activities to get to know each other
- 🪩 A gala evening !
