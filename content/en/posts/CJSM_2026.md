## Announcing the CJSM meeting

Registration for RCJSM 2026 is finally open! The meeting will take place at the Île de Loisirs des Boucles de Seine in the Yvelines, Île-de-France, from 23 to 27 March 2026.

All the information you need to register is on page 3 of the five-page flyer.

Key information:

- Complete this [Google Form](https://forms.gle/2GJ8SVuriNKn17jQ6).
- You must be an SFSM member for 2026 to attend RCJSM. [Remember to renew your SFSM membership](https://www.helloasso.com/associations/societe-francaise-de-spectrometrie-de-masse/adhesions/adhesion-sfsm-2025) for the new year.
- Registration is open until Monday 2 March at 12:00.
- Abstract submission closes on **27 February 2026 at 12:00** (pages 4 and 5 of the flyer). Please follow the required template carefully.
- As last year, places are limited and allocated on a first-come, first-served basis. Unfortunately, we had to turn down registrations in 2024.

Start: Monday 23 March 2026

- Departure at 15:00 from Gare Saint-Lazare.

End: Friday 27 March 2026

- Departure from the accommodation at 13:00.

**The four course topics are:**

- Glycan and glycopeptide analysis with Leila Bechtella (Associate Professor, LAMBE, Univ. Évry) and David Ropartz (Research Engineer, BIBS, INRAE Nantes).
- Data processing and AI for metabolomics data with Etienne Thevenot and Sylvain Dechaumet, research engineers at LI-MS, CEA Saclay (INRAE, Univ. Paris-Saclay, MetaboHUB).
- High-resolution mass spectrometry (Orbitrap).
- Forensic science: revealing the invisible.

**Oral presentations:**

You can submit an abstract for either a standard oral presentation or a flash presentation. The programme includes 30 standard and 30 flash presentations.

Only 60 places are available, on a first-come, first-served basis! Last year, more than half the places were booked in under four hours, and the event was fully booked within a week of registration opening. Look out for the registration announcement email, expected next week.

For more information, read the flyer [here](/uploads/pdf/CJSM_RCJSM_Flyer_2026Annonce.pdf).
