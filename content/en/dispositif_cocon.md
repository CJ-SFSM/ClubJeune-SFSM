# Cocon listening and support programme

Academic research can sometimes be challenging and affect our mental wellbeing. Doubts, questioning ourselves, isolation, academic pressure or difficulties with supervision can leave us feeling overwhelmed or simply needing someone to talk to.

Because no one should have to face difficulties alone, the SFSM Young Scientists' Club aims to provide a listening and support space where people can talk in a safe, confidential and caring setting. It is open to anyone who would like to discuss difficulties encountered along the way, whether professional, interpersonal or personal.

Our goal is to break isolation, encourage peer discussion and offer human support based on mutual help and shared experience, both good and bad. Many of us have been through difficult periods: let's talk about them together. Sometimes a simple conversation can make all the difference. This programme offers attentive, respectful and compassionate listening without judgement. It is open to any young professional who needs it, including research engineers, academic or industry-funded CIFRE PhD students, and postdocs.

## Available resources

If you would like to talk or simply ask a question, please write to **cocon.dispositifecoute.sfsm@gmail.com**. Only the three programme coordinators have access to this inbox.

You can include the name of the person you would like to speak with (Gauthier, Clarisse or Alexia) in your email subject line. All conversations with your contact will remain confidential.

A list of resources is also available [here (in French)](/uploads/pdf/Liste_ressources_CJSM.pdf).
